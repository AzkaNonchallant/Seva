Amalfi Coast Escape

Experience the breathtaking beauty of Italy's most famous coastline. dramatic cliffs, vibrant villages, and azure waters await you on this unforgettable journey.

About This Journey

Embark on a spectacular 5-day escape along the legendary Amalfi Coast. This curated experience perfectly balances guided exploration with luxurious leisure time. Discover hidden coves by private boat, wander through the lemon-scented streets of Ravello, and indulge in world-class authentic Italian cuisine overlooking the Mediterranean.

Our handpicked boutique accommodations provide a tranquil haven after a day of discovery. Whether you're navigating the iconic curves of the coastal road or sipping limoncello as the sun dips below the horizon, every moment is designed to immerse you in the enchanting spirit of Campania.

Itinerary

1

Arrival in Naples & Transfer to Sorrento

Arrive in Naples and enjoy a private luxury transfer to your cliffside hotel in Sorrento. Spend the evening settling in and enjoying a welcome dinner with panoramic bay views.

2

The Path of the Gods & Positano

A morning hike along the famous 'Path of the Gods' offering unparalleled coastal views. Descend into vibrant Positano for a leisurely afternoon of shopping and beachside relaxation.

3

Private Boat Tour to Capri

Board a private classic Italian speedboat to the island of Capri. Explore the Blue Grotto, stroll through the Piazzetta, and enjoy secluded swimming spots away from the crowds.

4

Amalfi & Ravello Exploration

Visit the historic town of Amalfi and its stunning cathedral. Continue up to peaceful Ravello to wander through the exquisite gardens of Villa Rufolo with their iconic cliffside vistas.

5

Farewell & Departure

Enjoy a final leisurely Italian breakfast before your private transfer returns you to Naples airport for your onward journey.
